Your football, rated and sorted

The 2022 final or last Sunday’s 0-0: every match you watch finds its place in your diary.

Everything to rate your football, nothing more

  1. 01

    A rating from 1 to 10

    In half points, like the sports press. A slider that follows your finger, and a crowd of fans that gets louder as your rating climbs.

  2. 02

    Your football diary

    Every match you watched, sorted by month, with your average and your ratings breakdown.

  3. 03

    Your friends’ takes

    Two feeds side by side: “For you” to discover, “Following” for the people you know. Like, comment, reply.

  4. 04

    Lineups on the pitch

    Scorers, assists and substitutions, and your man of the match picked right on the pitch.

  5. 05

    Share to your story

    Your review as an image: story card, transparent sticker, on your own photo or a square for a post.

  6. 06

    Your teams, your reminders

    Follow your clubs and your national team. A reminder at kickoff, then the question that matters.

Frequently asked questions

What is Lucarne?

An iPhone app to rate the football matches you watch. You give a rating from 1 to 10, write your review and read your friends’ reviews. Like Letterboxd for films, but for football.

Is Lucarne free?

Yes, completely. A few discreet banner ads appear in lists, never full screen.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone, coming soon to the App Store, in English, French, Spanish and Italian. There is no Android version yet.

Which matches can I rate?

Over 50,000 matches: Ligue 1, the Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A and the Bundesliga since 2001, the Champions League since 2002, every World Cup since 1994, every Euro since 2004 and the Nations League since its creation.

How do I rate a match?

Slide your finger from 1 to 10, in half points. You can add a review, your man of the match picked from the lineup, and the day you watched it.

Who can see my reviews?

You choose, for each review: everyone, only your followers, or just you.
