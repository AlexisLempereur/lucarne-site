Purpose

Lucarne lets you rate the football matches you watch, write reviews, share them and follow other fans.

By creating an account, you accept these terms. If you do not accept them, do not use the app.

Your account

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account.

You are responsible for what you post from your account and for keeping your credentials confidential.

You can delete your account at any time from the settings, and choose at that point whether your reviews and comments stay visible anonymously or disappear with everything else.

Zero tolerance for abusive content and behaviour

No insulting, hateful, harassing, threatening or sexually explicit content, and no incitement to violence, is tolerated on Lucarne. Harassing another user is not tolerated either.

Every review, every comment and every account can be reported from the app. Every user can also block another: blocking is mutual and immediate.

Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Any content that breaks these rules is removed, and the account responsible may be suspended or deleted without notice or refund.

These rules are not negotiable. Posting on Lucarne means accepting them.

What you post

You remain the owner of your reviews and comments. You grant us the right to display them in the app, including in the share cards you generate.

Each review has its own visibility: public, visible to everyone; followers only; or private, visible to you alone. This is enforced on the server, not just in the display.

Only post content you have the right to share.
